Collection method

A semantic navigation layer, not another progress dashboard.

The navigator helps policy teams find source-linked indicator definitions across African and global policy frameworks. It does not calculate country performance, publish a national score, or replace the original official source.

How to use results

Start with discovery. Verify before use.

Semantic matches are leads for policy research. Open the supplied official source before adopting, citing, or operationalising an indicator.

Why this product exists

It connects definitions across sources.

Existing platforms often provide values, country comparisons, project maps, or sector-specific climate analytics. This tool focuses on the earlier step: finding comparable indicator definitions across frameworks from a plain-language policy need.
